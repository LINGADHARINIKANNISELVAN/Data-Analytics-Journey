# Vendor Cost Comparison for Procurement Decisions

## Problem Statement

The company purchases raw materials and products from multiple vendors to support its daily operations and supply chain requirements. These vendors differ in pricing, delivery time, reliability, and product quality. Choosing the right vendors is important because procurement decisions directly affect overall business costs and profitability.

Currently, management aims to reduce procurement costs to improve profit margins. However, focusing only on lower prices may result in compromises in product quality or supplier reliability. Poor-quality materials or delayed deliveries can disrupt operations, affect product standards, and impact customer satisfaction.

Vendor selection decisions are largely based on past relationships, familiarity, or informal experience with suppliers. While these relationships may be valuable, they do not provide a structured way to evaluate vendor performance using measurable factors.

The company therefore needs a data-driven comparison of vendors based on factors such as:

- Cost efficiency
- Delivery performance
- Product quality
- Purchasing volume
- Price savings

### Key Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

- Which vendors consistently offer competitive prices?
- Which vendors provide better delivery performance?
- Are higher-priced vendors providing better quality or reliability?
- Which vendors offer better overall value for the company?

### Objective

The objective of this project is to support **data-driven vendor selection and procurement optimization**.

By comparing vendors using consistent metrics, the analysis can help the company:

- Identify vendors offering better pricing without compromising quality
- Improve supplier negotiation strategies
- Optimize sourcing decisions
- Reduce overall procurement expenses

## Analysis Approach

**Problem → Data → Cleaning → Calculations → Analysis → Dashboard → Findings**

The analysis evaluates vendors using procurement and performance indicators such as:

- Total Spend
- Unit Cost
- Negotiated Cost
- Price Saving %
- Delivery Days
- Defective Units
- Defect Rate
- Order Quantity
- Order Status
- Compliance

## Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard was created to compare vendor performance and support procurement decision-making.

**Dashboard features include:**

- Vendor comparison
- Product analysis
- Order status analysis
- Compliance analysis
- Procurement KPIs
- Interactive slicers

## Key Findings

- **Vendor F** had the lowest cost and fastest delivery performance.
- **Vendor H** recorded the lowest defect rate at **1.07%**.
- **Vendor A** recorded the highest price saving at **4.14%**.
- **Vendor B** had the highest purchase quantity at **72,204 units**.
- **Vendor A** had the lowest purchase quantity at **55,294 units**.

These findings demonstrate how procurement data can be used to compare vendors across multiple dimensions rather than relying on price alone.
